# Roadmap

Job Review Kit is an early public alpha. The roadmap favors trustworthy local workflows
over autonomous application activity.

## Now: alpha hardening

- Expand fixture-based connector coverage as public ATS schemas change.
- Improve keyboard navigation, accessible status announcements, and small-screen review.
- Reduce false positives in skill, location, authorization, and sponsorship detection.
- Keep dependency, privacy, and synthetic-demo checks green for every release.

## Next: explainability and review quality

- Make score-weight configuration easier to understand and compare.
- Add richer duplicate and repost explanations without hiding uncertain matches.
- Surface confidence and source evidence consistently across every inferred attribute.
- Add exportable, private review summaries for candidate-controlled workflows.

## Later: safe extensibility

- Document a stable connector interface for additional public employer feeds.
- Support more evidence-preserving DOCX layouts without rewriting unsupported structures.
- Add optional local scheduling and notifications with explicit retention controls.

## Deliberate non-goals

- Automatic application submission or employer outreach
- Authenticated job-board scraping or access-control bypasses
- Inventing resume claims, skills, or employment evidence
- A hosted multi-user service without a separately reviewed security architecture

Priorities may change based on reproducible issues and privacy-safe contributions. See
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) before proposing a feature or sharing diagnostic data.
